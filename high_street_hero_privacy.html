High Street Hero — Privacy Notice

How we handle game results and optional contact information during our customer intelligence pilot.

Draft for compliance review · November 2026

Publication checklist: Replace the legal operator name, contact address and direct enquiry-form link; confirm data retention, supplier arrangements and cookie information before collecting real participants' details.

1. Who we are

High Street Hero is a free customer intelligence pilot operated under the trading name Business Intelligence Studio. Data controller: [Full legal name of operator]. Contact address: [Business contact address]. Privacy questions and requests can be submitted using our Your Enquiry form.

2. Information we collect

When enabled, the pilot may collect game category, evaluation measure, brand comparisons, scores, results, submission times, and limited security information. If you voluntarily provide contact details, we may also collect your name, email address, confirmation that you are 18 or over, and your separate report and marketing choices.

3. Why and how we use it

  • Pilot analytics: Analyse participant opinions and improve the game. Our proposed lawful basis is legitimate interests, subject to an appropriate balancing assessment.
  • Requested monthly reports: Email participant-based brand comparison reports only where you opt in; we will record and respect your choice.
  • Separate marketing: Send Business Intelligence Studio promotional messages only where you separately opt in.
  • Security: Detect and prevent misuse of the service, based on legitimate interests where appropriate.

Consent can be withdrawn at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing. Reports represent participant opinions, not independent brand audits.

4. Who processes information

Our pilot uses Google Sheets and Google Apps Script for storage and processing, and Cloudflare for security and traffic handling. We do not currently provide identifiable participant details to other businesses for their own marketing or analysis. Relevant provider contracts, transfers and safeguards must be verified before public collection.

5. Retention

We intend to retain contact details only while needed to provide requested communications, with a defined deletion period following unsubscribe. We will keep consent evidence only as necessary to demonstrate compliance, and identifiable game results only as long as necessary for pilot analysis. Properly anonymised aggregate results may be retained longer. Exact periods and deletion processes must be published and implemented before launch.

6. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to processing, and exercise other rights under UK GDPR. Use Your Enquiry to contact us. You may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

7. Cookies and technical processing

We will provide details of cookies and similar technologies used by the website and any choices required by law once the deployed services have been assessed.

8. Changes

We may update this notice as the pilot develops. We will display the latest version on this page and make material changes clear.
