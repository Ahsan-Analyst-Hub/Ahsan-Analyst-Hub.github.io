The participation rules and optional communications for our customer intelligence pilot.

Pilot terms: High Street Hero currently offers no lucky draw, guaranteed voucher, gift card or coupon. The monthly Brand Performance Report is optional and based on participant opinions.

1. About High Street Hero

High Street Hero is a free interactive customer intelligence pilot operated by [Full legal name of operator], trading as Business Intelligence Studio. Contact address: [Business contact address].

2. Participation

Participation is free and intended for people aged 18 or over. No purchase is required. Participants choose a retail category and comparison measure and select preferred brands. Please do not deliberately manipulate the game or submit false information.

3. Use and interpretation of results

We may analyse game scores to create aggregated comparisons and demonstrations of customer intelligence. Results reflect the opinions of participants and do not represent independently verified brand performance or endorsements by the brands mentioned.

4. Optional monthly Brand Performance Report

Participants may choose to receive a monthly report by email. This requires a separate affirmative choice and may be withdrawn at any time. Report availability and content depend on sufficient useful pilot data; a particular report or ranking is not guaranteed.

5. Separate marketing choice

Consent to receive Business Intelligence Studio's promotional emails is separate and optional. You may withdraw report and marketing permissions independently. We will provide an easy way to unsubscribe in each relevant email.

6. No prizes or reward promotion

There is no lucky draw or promised gift card, shopping voucher or coupon under these pilot terms. Any future promotion will be separately explained and governed by its own published conditions.

See our Privacy Notice for details of personal information, legal bases, service providers, retention and rights.

We may improve, pause or discontinue pilot features, subject to applicable law and existing commitments. For questions, use our Your Enquiry form.
